KEY FEATURES
Built for Bookshops, Designed for Staff

Everything needed to run a bookshop's day-to-day, in one system.

Inventory Management

Track stock levels per book, get automatic low-stock and out-of-stock alerts, and never oversell what isn't on the shelf.

Sales Management

Record a sale with multiple books in one transaction — totals are calculated and stock is reduced automatically.

Customer Management

Keep a record of customers and their full purchase history, searchable in seconds.

Book Catalog

Organize books by category, author or title, with cover images and instant search.

Reports & Analytics

Sales and inventory reports with real revenue, top-selling books, and stock value — no manual spreadsheets.

Secure & Reliable

Every action happens behind staff login, with stock changes protected by database transactions.
